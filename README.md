### Hi, I'm Rifat Islam Rupok

Independent researcher based in Winnipeg, working where political economy, international relations and data meet. I hold an MSS in Peace & Conflict Studies (University of Dhaka), and I like turning messy questions into clear evidence and small, useful tools.

**Research interests:** South Asian geopolitics · India–Bangladesh relations · US–China rivalry in South & Southeast Asia · education and technology access for marginalised communities · political economy
