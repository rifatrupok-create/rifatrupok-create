03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Analytical mind.
Human perspective.

I’m interested in how systems shape people’s lives — and how better evidence, clearer thinking, and thoughtful tools can improve them.

My path has taken me from peace and conflict research in Bangladesh to community-focused development in Winnipeg. Across those settings, the thread is the same: listen closely, understand the context, and make the work useful.

Outside my day-to-day work, I write about geopolitics, build small digital tools, and follow my curiosity into political economy, history, and mathematics.
